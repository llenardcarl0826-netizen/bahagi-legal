Delete your Bahagi account

Bahagi (Android app: ph.bahagi.app) · Last updated: October 13, 2026

Option 1: Delete it yourself in the app (fastest)

  1. Open Bahagi and sign in.
  2. Go to Profile → Security → Delete Account.
  3. Confirm with your password, or by signing in again with Google or Facebook.

Before you can delete, every group must be settled up (your balance must be zero), and any payment must be confirmed by the other person. The app tells you which group still needs settling.

Option 2: Ask us to delete it

If you cannot open the app or no longer have it installed, email us from the address you signed up with:

Email a deletion request

Include your name and the email address on the account. We may ask one question to confirm it is really you. We will process the request within 30 days. Balances in your groups still have to be settled with the other members, because we cannot erase money someone else is owed.

Signed in with Facebook or Google?

Deleting your Bahagi account removes the data described below. It does not delete your Facebook or Google account. You can also remove Bahagi from your Facebook Settings → Apps and Websites or from your Google account’s Third-party connections page.

What is deleted

  • Your profile: name, email, profile photo link, payment method labels, reminder settings and push-notification tokens.
  • Your @username, which becomes available to others.
  • Your sign-in account (Firebase Authentication).
  • Invites sent to or from you, and the entry that let people find you by email.
  • Your membership in every group. A group where you are the only member is deleted completely. If you were the group admin, admin passes to another member.

What stays, and why

  • Shared expense and chat history in groups that still have other members. It belongs to the group as a whole, so those members keep their records. Your name may still appear on entries you made there.
  • Images and voice messages you uploaded to a group may remain on our image host (Cloudinary) while that group exists. Email us if you want specific files removed.

To have your name removed from the shared records as well, email supportbahagi@gmail.com and tell us which groups.
