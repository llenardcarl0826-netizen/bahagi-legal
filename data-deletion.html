How to delete your Bahagi data

Last updated: September 20, 2026

Option 1: delete your account in the app

  1. Open Bahagi and go to Profile, then Security & privacy.
  2. Settle up any outstanding balances in your groups. The app will not delete an account that still owes or is owed money.
  3. Tap Delete account and confirm. If you signed in with Google or Facebook, you will be asked to sign in with it once more to confirm it is you.

What is deleted

  • Your profile: name, email, profile photo link, username, payment details and notification tokens.
  • Your membership in every group, and any pending invitations to or from you.
  • Your sign-in (the account itself).

What can remain

  • Expenses, payment records and chat messages you added to a group that still has other members stay in that group's history, because they are part of a shared record.
  • Images and voice notes you shared are stored with our storage provider and may remain until we remove them. You can ask us to remove them (see below).

Option 2: ask us to delete it

If you cannot use the app, email [supportbahagi@gmail.com] from the address on your account, or tell us which name and email you signed up with. We will act on your request within [3] days.

If you signed in with Facebook

Removing Bahagi from your Facebook settings (Settings, then Apps and Websites) stops Facebook sharing new information with the app, but it does not delete your Bahagi account. To delete your Bahagi data, use one of the two options above.
