Bahagi Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 20, 2026

Bahagi ("the app") helps friends and housemates split shared bills. This page explains what information the app collects, how it is used, and the choices you have. The app is run by [Llenard Carl T. Alcayna].

Information we collect

  • Account details: your name, your email address (if your sign-in method provides one), your profile picture, and the username you choose.
  • Signing in with Google or Facebook: we receive your name, your email address (from Facebook only if your account has one and you allow it), your profile picture, and a unique ID from that provider. We never receive your Google or Facebook password, and we never post anything on your behalf.
  • Payment details you add: for example a GCash, Maya or bank account name or number. You choose whether to add these.
  • Group activity: groups you create or join, their members, expenses and balances, payment and settlement requests, receipt images, and chat messages, photos, GIFs and voice notes.
  • Device information: a push-notification token so we can send you reminders and alerts.
  • Camera, microphone and photos: only when you choose to take a photo, record a voice note or attach an image. Reading text from a receipt happens on your device.

How we use it

We use this information to run the app: to sign you in, show your groups and balances, let members chat and settle up, and send notifications. We do not sell your information and we do not use it for advertising.

Who can see it

  • Other members of your groups can see your name, photo, your activity in the group, your messages, and any payment details you have added. People who are not in your groups cannot see your profile details.
  • Service providers that store and deliver the app's data on our behalf: Google Firebase (sign-in, database and push notifications) and Cloudinary (storage for images and voice notes).
  • Sign-in providers (Google and Facebook) know that you used them to sign in to Bahagi.

Keeping your data

We keep your data while your account is active. You can delete your account at any time (see below). Data is sent over encrypted connections and access is limited by account and group membership. No online service is perfectly secure, so please share payment details only with people you trust.

Deleting your data

In the app, go to Profile, then Security & privacy, then Delete account. You will need to settle up any outstanding balances first. Full instructions, and what is removed, are on our data deletion page.

Children

Bahagi is not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy we will update the date at the top of this page.
