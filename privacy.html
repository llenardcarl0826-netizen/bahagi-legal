Privacy Policy

Bahagi (Android app: ph.bahagi.app) · Last updated: October 13, 2026

1. Who we are

Bahagi is a bill-splitting app operated by Llenard Carl T. Alcayna, based in Ifugao, Philippines. Questions about this policy or your data: supportbahagi@gmail.com.

2. What we collect

  • Account information: your name and email address. If you sign in with Google or Facebook, we receive the name, email address and profile photo your account shares with us. If you sign up with a password, the password is handled and encrypted by Firebase Authentication; we never see or store it.
  • Content you create: groups, expenses, amounts, who paid and who owes, settle-up and payment-confirmation records, and chat messages, photos, GIFs and voice messages you send in a group.
  • Payment method labels you add (for example “GCash”, “Maya” or “BPI”). We store only the label you type, never an account number, card number or any payment credential.
  • Photos and receipts you choose to upload (profile photo, group photo, receipt images, chat images).
  • A device push-notification token, so we can send you reminders and updates.
  • Basic technical data such as app version and device platform, generated when the app runs.

3. Device permissions

  • Camera: to take a profile, receipt or chat photo, or to scan a group invite QR code.
  • Microphone: only when you record a voice message.
  • Photos: only the images you pick yourself with the system photo picker. The app does not scan your library.
  • Notifications: to deliver reminders and updates you have enabled.

Reading text from a receipt photo is done on your device (Google ML Kit text recognition). The photo is not sent to us for that step.

4. What we do not collect

We do not collect bank account numbers, card numbers, GCash/Maya credentials, your contacts, or your location. Bahagi does not process payments; it only tracks who owes what.

5. How we use your data

  • To create and operate your account.
  • To show your groups, expenses and balances.
  • To let the people you share a group with see the group name, members, shared expenses and chat.
  • To send notifications and reminders.

We do not sell your data and we do not use it for advertising.

6. Who we share it with

These providers process data on our behalf to run the app:

  • Google Firebase (Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Cloud Messaging): sign-in, app data storage and push notifications.
  • Cloudinary: storage of images and voice messages you upload.
  • Giphy: if you use the GIF picker in chat, the words you search for are sent to Giphy to return results.
  • Google and Meta (Facebook): only if you choose to sign in with them.

Other members of a group can see what you add to that group. We do not share your data with anyone else.

7. How long we keep it

We keep your account and app data while your account is active. You can delete your account at any time, in the app or from the account deletion page. Shared expense and chat history in a group stays for the remaining members, because it belongs to the group as a whole; the page above explains exactly what is removed and what stays.

8. Your rights

Under the Philippine Data Privacy Act of 2012 (RA 10173) you have the right to be informed, to access your data, to object to certain processing, to ask for correction or erasure, to data portability, and to complain to the National Privacy Commission (privacy.gov.ph) if you believe your rights have been violated. To use any of these rights, email supportbahagi@gmail.com.

9. Children

Bahagi is not directed at children under 18, and we do not knowingly collect data from anyone under that age.

10. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, the new version and its date will be posted on this page.
